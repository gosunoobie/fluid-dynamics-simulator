# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — Standalone project export

### Project packaging

- Exported the interactive simulator as standalone HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Separated material metadata, simulation, canvas rendering, and application controls.
- Added a dependency-free local server, automated checks and tests, and repository workflow configuration.
- Added setup, architecture, material, development, testing, and contribution documentation.

### Included simulation

- Blank canvas, material selection, pointer painting, accessible Add action, pause/resume, reset, and live pixel count.
- Sand, stone, water, lava, smoke, wood, fire, acid, oil, gunpowder, ice, snow, metal, ash, and charcoal.
- Internal steam and debris, an eraser, and a manual explosion tool.
- Gravity, momentum, piles, categorical density displacement, and supported liquid splashes.
- Inelastic contacts that prevent falling particles from bouncing upward against one another.
- Gunpowder chain reactions, blast damage, and outward particle momentum.
- Cold-material melting, water/lava cooling, and local metal heat conduction.
- Wood charring into charcoal and charcoal burning into ash.
- Natural material colors, pixel textures, light/dark themes, and reduced-motion startup behavior.

This is the first packaged release. Earlier material additions were developed as successive interactive prototypes rather than separately versioned releases.
